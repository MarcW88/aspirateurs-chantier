# AUDIT — `/comparatifs/meilleur-aspirateur-de-chantier/`

Date: 2026-09-09
Mode: `AUDIT`
Decision: `DEEP_REWRITE`
Confidence: 95%
Indexing: `KEEP_NOINDEX`

## Intent

The URL has a legitimate umbrella role: help a user orient between fundamentally different construction-vacuum needs before drilling into class M, cordless, wet/dry, compact or professional pages.

## Preserve

- the distinction between raw suction figures and actual suitability;
- the warning that a consumer wet/dry vacuum is not a substitute for a safety-class machine;
- manufacturer-source discipline and desk-research disclosure;
- the idea that one broad query needs conditional recommendations.

## Blockers

1. **Exact candidate clone with `/aspirateur-chantier-puissant/`.** Both pages use Bosch GAS 35 M AFC, Kärcher NT 30/1 Tact Te M ACD and Kärcher WD 7 Control. The difference is mainly weights and winner framing.
2. **Scope too narrow for the umbrella page.** Three products, two from Kärcher, cannot credibly represent the site's broadest buying decision without a documented candidate screen.
3. **Universal weighted ranking is the wrong abstraction.** The candidates span a consumer wet/dry machine and class-M professional machines; a single score hides scenario gates.
4. **Legacy cloned architecture.** The page uses the same ranking/method/models/choice/limits structure as the seven other legacy comparisons.
5. **Cross-brand measurement risk.** Airflow/depression are included in the score despite measurement-point comparability not being demonstrated.

## Secondary improvements

- make the page a routing/decision hub rather than a long product podium;
- ensure each recommended profile hands off to the specialist comparison or usage page;
- broaden brand/category consideration before selecting representative products;
- title/meta can remain close to current intent if the page keeps the umbrella role.

## Missing data

- GSC / semantic opportunity by sub-intent was not available in this audit;
- no exhaustive 2026 market screen has yet been persisted for this URL.

## Next step

Rebuild as a scenario-led umbrella: identify the major decision branches first (risk/class, wet-dry cleaning, cordless mobility, compactness, professional continuity), then choose representative products only after the branches are fixed. Do not reuse the powerful page's candidate set or ranking.
